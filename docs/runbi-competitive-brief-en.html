Why the Window Is Real (and Why It Closes)

  • The interaction is proven: PopClip/Bob have charged for "select → act → replace" on macOS for 15 years. Nobody built it for Windows.
  • Today's four-element gap: every international player misses Chinese (QuillBot 6 lang, no zh; Grammarly EN-first; Wordtune EN-only; DeepL Write zh but whole-text workbench, not selection). Domestic players are free-tier, browser-locked or input-method-locked.
  • The threat: Raycast shipped a Windows version. QuillBot's In-Page Editing proves the interaction at 35M users — don't bet on it never adding Chinese. Estimated window: 6–12 months.
  • Privacy wedge: BYOK (bring your own key) means data never touches our cloud — the wedge both for C-end trust and B2B "no data leaves the company" deals.
